01 The purpose

Innovation begins with a problem worth solving.

The I.T.S Dental College Incubation Centre is envisioned as a collaborative environment where dental and oral-health challenges can be explored, tested, and developed into practical solutions.

By bringing together clinical perspectives, scientific knowledge, technology, and entrepreneurial thinking, the Centre aims to support innovators moving from early ideas towards validation and potential real-world adoption.

03 The incubation journey

From clinical challenge to real-world impact.

A considered path from understanding a need to exploring responsible routes towards adoption.

01

Identify

Discover an unmet need in dentistry, oral healthcare, or clinical practice.

02

Explore

Understand the problem, users, alternatives, and opportunities.

03

Develop

Shape the idea into a concept, prototype, or initial solution.

04

Validate

Gather relevant feedback, assess feasibility, and refine.

05

Translate

Explore models, IP, quality, safety, and regulatory considerations.

06

Scale

Pursue appropriate routes towards adoption and partnerships.

THE JOURNEY IS ITERATIVE. OUTCOMES DEPEND ON CONTEXT, EVIDENCE, AND READINESS.
